On Mac, **Outdent** and **Indent** follow the list buttons in the composer's format bar, **Format › Increase Indent** (Command-]) and **Decrease Indent** (Command-[) do the same, and Tab and Shift-Tab do it inside a list. Lists nest three levels deep and each change is one Undo step (#2597, part of #1679).
