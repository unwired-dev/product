iPhone and iPad: the Save your Recovery Key step offers **Copy** and **Save to Files** under the key. Copy puts the key on this device's clipboard only, where it stays for two minutes; Save to Files hands a plain-text file with the key to the system's save panel and keeps no copy. Neither confirms the key (#2616).
