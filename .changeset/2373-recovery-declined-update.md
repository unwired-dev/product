A composer whose Draft update the store declines, as when its sender moved into another Mail Profile, no longer counts that version as saved: Save keeps the composer open and its recovery record stays. On the Mac, an idle Inbox window offers a recovery record once a mailbox moves into its Profile (#2373, part of #1989).
