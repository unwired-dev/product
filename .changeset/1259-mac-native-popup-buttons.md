Mac pop-up controls are now native AppKit pop-up buttons. Settings choices, SMTP security, the composer's From and Text style show their current choice and open their menu over it; the reader's Folders and the composer's actions and Writing tools are pull-down buttons that show their symbol. AppKit draws their pressed, focused and disabled states, and VoiceOver reads each as one pop-up or menu button.
