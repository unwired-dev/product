A narrow iPad composer, such as the 442pt Portrait reader, folds its format bar as the Portrait frames draw it: each crowded section ends with an overflow menu. More Formatting holds Underline, Strikethrough and Inline code; More Lists and Links holds Numbered List, Add Link or Edit Link (whose Link Editor opens from the menu), Undo and Redo; More Files and Templates holds Attach Photo, Insert Image, Paste Image and Templates (#2357, part of #2095).
