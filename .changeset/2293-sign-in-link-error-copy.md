Mac, iPad and iPhone explain a Google or Apple sign-in link that did not finish: offline, the message says the device is offline and to choose Link … Sign-In again once it's online; any other failure says linking didn't finish and names the same button, instead of "could not be linked … Try again". Neither claims the Unwired account is unchanged, since a link can commit while its reply is lost (#2293, part of #2006).
