On iPhone, Reply in the reader grows a floating glass sheet out of the button (#969). It shows the sender's initials, name and message preview beside a Close button, above Reply, Reply All and Forward tiles, and shrinks back toward the button when closed. Reader menu panels now ease in from their toolbar and fade out on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Folders on iPhone opens a Move picker that grows out of the button the same way: the mailbox's address, the message's sender and subject, folders with their role symbols (the current one unavailable) and a search field at the bottom. With Reduce Motion, menus and sheets only fade.
