On iPhone, Reply in the reader grows a floating glass sheet out of the button (#969). It shows the sender's initials, name and message preview beside a Close button, then Reply, Reply All, Forward and Archive tiles and the Thread's other organizing actions in grouped rows, beside the toolbar's More, and shrinks back toward the button when closed. Reader menu panels and the composer's pop-up menus now ease in from their control and fade out on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Folders on iPhone opens a Move picker that grows out of the button the same way: the mailbox's address, the message's sender and subject, folders with their role symbols (the current one unavailable) and a search field at the bottom. With Reduce Motion, menus and sheets only fade.

Move search keeps its results visible in landscape with the software keyboard open.
