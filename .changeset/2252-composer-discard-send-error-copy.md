Mac, iPad and iPhone explain a composer Discard or Send that storage refused: a refused Discard says the Draft wasn’t discarded and stays open, and to choose Try again or keep editing, and a refused Send says the message wasn’t sent because this device couldn’t store it for sending, that it stays open, and to choose Send again, instead of only "could not … Try again" (#2252, part of #2006).
