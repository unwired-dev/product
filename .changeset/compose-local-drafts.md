Compose rich-text Drafts on iPhone, iPad and Mac. **New Message** opens an adaptive composer with the sending mailbox always shown, validated To, Cc and Bcc recipients that refuse duplicates, a subject and a formatted body with headings, lists, quotes, code and inline marks, Markdown shortcuts, and Undo and Redo. Every edit autosaves in order to encrypted storage on this device for the signed-in Product Account; a Draft that cannot be saved stays open. Drafts are listed apart from received mail and reopen after an interruption or relaunch without being sent. A Draft whose mailbox is removed or needs Gmail access again keeps it until another mailbox is chosen. Signing out or deleting the account removes its Drafts from this device.
