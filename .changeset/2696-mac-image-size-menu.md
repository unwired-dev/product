On the Mac, Control-clicking an inline image's row in the composer opens an Image Size submenu: Small · 320 px, Medium · 640 px, Large · 1024 px and Original, with a checkmark at the current size, then Remove Image; VoiceOver offers the sizes as actions. A size applies to every use of the image in the body as one Undo step (#2696, part of #1735).
