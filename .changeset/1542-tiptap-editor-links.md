The registration-draft-editor Tiptap evaluation editor keeps a Draft's links through editing, saving and reopening, and shows linked text in the link color. Text typed inside a link joins it and text typed at its edges stays unlinked. A pasted link keeps its destination only when it is a web, mail or telephone address the Draft admits as written; any other pasted link keeps only its text.
