Search Gmail, Load More and Try Again in online search, and Retry in the reader, are text actions on iPhone, iPad and Mac: they fill with the subtle selection color while pressed and, on the Mac, show the 2pt accent ring in place of the system focus ring. Message link controls fill with the subtle selection color while pressed, and on the Mac their accent border replaces the system focus ring.
