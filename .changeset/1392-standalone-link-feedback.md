“Search mail servers for …”, “More from mail servers” and “Search mail servers again” in online search, and “Try again” in the reader, are text actions on iPhone, iPad and Mac: they fill with the subtle selection color while pressed and, on the Mac, show the 2pt accent ring in place of the system focus ring. Message link controls fill with the subtle selection color while pressed, and on the Mac their accent border replaces the system focus ring.
