Mac, iPad and iPhone explain a Writing tools result that didn’t finish for a reason the app can’t name: the Writing tools panel now says the suggestion didn’t finish, that your text is unchanged, and to choose Try Again to get writing help, instead of only "The suggestion could not be created." When a long reply leaves no room for the quoted message, Suggest Reply now says so and suggests shortening the reply, instead of the same failure message without a Try Again button (#2307, #2308, part of #2006).
