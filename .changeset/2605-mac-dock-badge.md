On the Mac, **Settings › Notifications** offers a **Dock badge** pop-up with **Off** and **Unread in Inbox**. It starts **Off** and stays on this Mac. With **Unread in Inbox**, the Dock icon shows how many unread messages this Mac lists in the Inbox of every open mailbox, and the badge is removed when the choice is **Off**, notifications are turned off, the last mailbox is removed or the person signs out. While notifications are off, the choice stays, dimmed (#2605, part of #1723).
