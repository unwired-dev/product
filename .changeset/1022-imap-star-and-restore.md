Star IMAP mail with the server's `\Flagged` flag from the reader, Inbox rows, context menus and the Command Palette on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and see stars set in other mail apps. Mail in an IMAP connection's Trash or Spam folder offers Move to Inbox instead of Archive, Move to Trash and Report spam, as Gmail's does.
