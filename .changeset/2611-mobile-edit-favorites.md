On iPhone and iPad, **Edit** in Mailboxes, beside Account on iPhone and the sidebar toggle on iPad, lets Favorites show or hide each row and reorder them until **Done**; All Inboxes stays shown. The rows are the Profile's synchronized Inbox preference the Mac already edits, hiding the selected Unified Mailbox selects the first row still shown, and the Command Palette opens each Unified Mailbox, leaving a reader or composer that covers the list first, and, while the iPad sidebar shows, Edit Favorites (#2611, part of #2106). Edit also shows or hides each Mailbox Connection and its folders and reorders them: connections in the Profile's shared order, where a refused order says so, and each connection's visibility and folders in synchronized Inbox preference fields of their own (`showSection`, `changeFolders` and `sections`, which is undefined until read and after a failed read; `sectionShown`, `sectionFolders`, `folderRows`, `showFolder`, `moveFolder`, and `showFavorite`, so a tap stores the opposite of what its row showed even when another write lands first, and a tap on a connection's Inbox or on All Inboxes, which stay shown, writes nothing). A hidden connection leaves All Inboxes and its search, and posts no New Mail Notifications on any device, the Mac included, as each delivery pulls the preferences from Private Sync once the notification settings are read, then checks the Profile its connection is in in the same step that hands them to the host (`arrived` takes `settle` and `eligible(profile)`), so a hide or move counts for every delivery checked after this device stores it, and none posts while that pull fails, the Profile's record does not open, or another device's write overtakes every pass (`pulled`); until the preferences are read, and while a later read fails, none posts, even one already pending. Hiding the selected connection or folder selects All Inboxes. A drag scrolls Mailboxes near its edges once the row moves, and changes nothing when another device reorders the rows meanwhile: `arrangeMailboxes` takes the order it was made `from` and writes nothing when the stored order differs, and an arrangement made during foreground verification is stored once it finishes; a Favorites or folder move onto rows whose order this device stored after showing them writes nothing either, as a `changeFavorites` or `changeFolders` change that returns undefined writes nothing. After a relaunch, All Inboxes lists no account's mail, nor Mailboxes any account, and Edit, from its button or the Command Palette, is unavailable, until the preferences are read, and All Inboxes names no hidden account, not even one waiting for or needing sign-in, nor counts one in its Sync Status Capsule, its subtitle, Filters, loading state, search progress or answer, or pull to refresh, and an account's own Inbox is named as its scope; All Inboxes takes its loading state, readiness and row motion only from the accounts it shows, none of them hidden, so a row leaving as an account is hidden leaves at once; while the preferences are read it shows it is loading, and after a failed read (`unreadable`, for each account whose read failed) it says so with Try Again, which, like pull to refresh, reads them again, a pull while they are read or unreadable then synchronizing the accounts they show; with every account hidden, even one waiting for sign-in, it names itself, not the provider, and shows No Mail and search answers once the preferences are read, without waiting for any account to list; a drag whose account another device removes ends with it; signed out, `sections` hides none. `sidebarCommands` takes `unified` as optional, as it takes `favorites`.
