On iPhone and iPad, **Edit** in Mailboxes, beside Account on iPhone and the sidebar toggle on iPad, lets Favorites show or hide each row and reorder them until **Done**; All Inboxes stays shown. The rows are the Profile's synchronized Inbox preference the Mac already edits, hiding the selected Unified Mailbox selects the first row still shown, and the Command Palette opens each Unified Mailbox, leaving a reader or composer that covers the list first, and, while the iPad sidebar shows, Edit Favorites (#2611, part of #2106). Edit also shows or hides each Mailbox Connection and its folders and reorders them: connections in the Profile's shared order, where a refused order says so, and each connection's visibility and folders in synchronized Inbox preference fields of their own (`showSection`, `changeFolders` and `sections`, which is undefined until read; `sectionShown`, `sectionFolders`, `folderRows`, `toggleFolder`, `moveFolder`). A hidden connection leaves All Inboxes and its search, and posts no New Mail Notifications on any device, the Mac included; until the preferences are read, and while a later read fails, none posts. Hiding the selected connection or folder selects All Inboxes. A drag scrolls Mailboxes near its edges once the row moves, and changes nothing when another device reorders the rows meanwhile: `arrangeMailboxes` takes the order it was made `from` and writes nothing when the stored order differs, and an arrangement made during foreground verification is stored once it finishes. `inboxPreferences.load` resolves whether the preferences were read. `sidebarCommands` takes `unified` as optional, as it takes `favorites`.
