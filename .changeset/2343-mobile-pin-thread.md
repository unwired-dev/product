On iPad and iPhone, Pin and Unpin a message's Thread: iPad's reader toolbar shows Pin before More, iPhone lists it in the reader's More menu and Reply sheet, and both offer it in the row and reader menus, VoiceOver's row actions and the Command Palette. Every row of a pinned Thread shows a pin and VoiceOver says Pinned. Pins stay on this device and never change a provider flag or star (#2343, part of #2084).
