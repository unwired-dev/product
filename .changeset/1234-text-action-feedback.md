Text actions in the reader and composer, such as Summarize, Translate, Load Images, Cc/Bcc, Retry and the Outbox's Undo and Edit, now show feedback while pressed and focused. Pressing one shades it with the subtle selection color until release or cancel. Keyboard focus draws a 2 pt accent ring around it, and composer text actions can now be reached by keyboard. Disabled actions stay grey. The labels keep their positions, sizes and hit targets.
