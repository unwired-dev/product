Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 meeting invitations show the invitation card on Mac, iPhone and iPad, read from the message's MIME, and **Accept**, **Maybe** or **Decline** sends one reply through the owning Microsoft connection. **Check Again** finds an unconfirmed reply in Sent Items by its Message-ID, and the card names the provider Outlook.com or Microsoft 365 (#2703).
