An older revision of a Gmail invitation offers **Show Latest** on Mac, iPhone and iPad, which opens the message carrying the newest revision this device answered. A recorded response now keeps the message it answered; records saved before it have no **Show Latest**. A Gmail online search result that Gmail later deletes no longer stays available to the reader.
