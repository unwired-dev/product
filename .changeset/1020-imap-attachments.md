List, download, open and share received attachments of IMAP mail on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with the same rows, limits and encrypted storage as Gmail. Downloads read the message source again at its folder's UIDVALIDITY, and a new UIDVALIDITY deletes saved attachments of the folder's earlier messages, including downloads in flight.

Gmail, Microsoft and IMAP Inboxes now share one attachment download implementation.
