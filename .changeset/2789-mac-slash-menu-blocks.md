On the Mac, typing `/` first in a body block opens the Slash Command Menu with its Basic Blocks, each ending with its Markdown marker. Typing filters the rows, Up and Down move the highlight, Return, Tab or a click sets the caret's block as one edit that one Undo reverts, and Escape, a caret move or leaving the body closes the menu.
