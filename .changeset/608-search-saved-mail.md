Search the Inbox mail saved on iPhone, iPad and Mac by sender name, address or subject, across all Gmail mailboxes or within one. Search reads only this device's encrypted cache, so it works offline and asks Gmail nothing. Each result opens in the reader and says whether its body is already saved on this device or downloads from Gmail when opened. A slower answer for an earlier search never replaces the current results, and a removed mailbox's mail leaves them at once.
