On iPhone and iPad, Settings has a **Reading** page under a new Mail group. **Message Zoom** (100% by default) zooms each received formatted message as you read it, and **Fit Wide Messages to the Screen** (off by default) shrinks a message wider than the reader to its width instead of scrolling sideways. Both choices stay on this device; app text keeps following the system Text Size, and the message and your Drafts don't change (#2483, part of #1728).
