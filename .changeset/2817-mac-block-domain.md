On the Mac, the reader's sender menu offers Block Domain… under Block Sender…: its sheet blocks the sender's exact domain for the Mail Profile, with its subdomains or alone, and can exempt the person's contacts there. Settings › Blocked Senders lists each Mail Profile's Domains before its Addresses, shows an `xn--` domain with the characters it displays, and − unblocks the selected domain.
