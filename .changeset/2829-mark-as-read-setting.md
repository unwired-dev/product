Settings › Reading on iPhone, iPad and Mac offers **Mark as Read** for each Mail Profile: **Immediately** (the default), **After 2 seconds** or **Manually**. The reader marks Gmail, Microsoft and IMAP mail read on opening as the Profile chose, and the choice syncs to the Profile's other devices (#2829).
