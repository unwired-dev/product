On the Mac, toasts are Liquid Glass capsules instead of deep graphite: 48pt tall with a 24pt radius and the glass's own shadow, the symbol and message in the primary text color and the action in the accent ink in Light. The refused Undo or Edit toast shows the frames' shorter "Couldn’t return “<subject>” to Drafts."; VoiceOver still hears the full sentence (#2751, part of #2104).
