iPad follows the Figma frames: columns start beneath the status bar with our own sidebar toggle, a floating sidebar panel, one Command input beside the reader, a native More menu, and Settings with Close at the top left. Dark Liquid Glass takes the frames' near-opaque fill, the reading column fills beneath the status bar and home indicator, New Message opens as a centred card while a reply docks beneath its message with From and Send below its fields, and Move opens a searchable popover beneath the toolbar's Move.
