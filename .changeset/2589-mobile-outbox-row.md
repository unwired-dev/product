On iPhone and iPad the Outbox is a row after Sent, in Mailboxes on iPhone and in the sidebar on iPad, while the Mail Profile has a message in it, instead of rows at the top of the Inbox column. Selecting it, or View in Outbox, lists the messages under the heading Outbox with a subtitle counting those waiting and those not sent; when the last one leaves, the list returns to All Inboxes. VoiceOver hears where each message stands whichever list shows (#2589, part of #2101).
