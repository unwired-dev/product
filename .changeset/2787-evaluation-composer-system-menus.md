The evaluation composer opens Attach on iPhone, and Writing tools on iPhone and iPad, as system menus, as the regular composer does.
On iPhone, both menus end at the trailing edge and bottom of the composer controls, which hide while a menu is open, and list their actions top-down. The iPhone compose sheet shows the system grabber. On iPhone, the evaluation composer's header, From and body sit where the frames place them, and its body text uses the primary text colour. Paste Image shows two pages. iPad and Mac keep their sheet top and editor text as before.
