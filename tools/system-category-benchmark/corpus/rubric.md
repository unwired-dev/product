# System Category labeling rubric (labels v1)

Each message receives zero or more of the five System Categories, decided
independently. A message with none is in Uncategorized State. Decide from the
sender, recipients, mailing-list headers, subject and body, as the recipient
would read the message. Judge the message's purpose, not isolated words.

## Orders

A transactional message about a purchase or payment the recipient or their
organization made, owes or receives: order and booking confirmations, invoices,
receipts, payment confirmations, reminders and failures, shipping and delivery
notices, cancellations, refunds and returns.

Not Orders: marketing that advertises products (Newsletters & Promotions);
account, password or security notices without a purchase; energy or financial
trading deal confirmations, trade tickets and internal business reports.

## Newsletters & Promotions

Bulk editorial or commercial mail: newsletters and digests (including internal
company newsletters and mailing-list digests), marketing campaigns, coupons,
sales, product and service announcements, advertising and event marketing.

Not Newsletters & Promotions: an individual person's post to a discussion
mailing list; one-off organizational announcements that are neither editorial
newsletters nor marketing.

## Invites

A message whose purpose is to invite the recipient to a meeting or event, or to
ask for an RSVP or attendance reply: meeting requests, calendar invitations,
party, lunch, conference or webinar invitations, including rescheduled meetings
that ask for attendance.

Not Invites: calendar acceptance or decline notices, meeting minutes, and
messages that only mention a meeting.

## Flights

A message about the recipient's own air travel: itineraries containing flight
segments from airlines or travel agencies, e-tickets, check-in and boarding
information, and flight status or schedule changes.

Not Flights: airfare marketing (Newsletters & Promotions only); hotel, rail or
car bookings without a flight; a casual mention of a flight in personal
correspondence.

## People

The fallback for person-to-person correspondence a human wrote for direct
conversation with the recipients: individual emails, replies and small-group
discussion. Assign People only when none of the four categories above applies.

Not People: bulk mail, newsletters, transactional or automated notifications,
announcements broadcast to large distribution lists (for example all-employee
memos), automated messages written to look personal, and individual posts
delivered through public discussion mailing lists.

## Exclude

Mark a message excluded instead of labeling it when its text is empty or
garbled after extraction, it is primarily not English, or it is spam or
phishing misfiled as legitimate mail.

## Recorded gaps

- Discussion mailing-list posts are human-written but not direct conversation;
  they are Uncategorized, and no category represents them.
- Trading confirmations and internal business reports are transactional but
  not purchases; they are Uncategorized.
- Airfare marketing is Newsletters & Promotions only.
- Corporate asset sales and change orders discussed between colleagues: the
  agreement check split between Orders and People, and the rubric does not
  settle it.
- The real-public sources are English. Czech examples are synthetic, so they
  are not independent real-world Czech validation.
