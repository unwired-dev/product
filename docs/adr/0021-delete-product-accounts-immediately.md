# Delete Product Accounts immediately

Deleting a Product Account is immediate and irreversible after recent authentication and explicit confirmation, with no recovery grace period. The operation deletes backend operational account data, encrypted Product Sync payloads, and push routes and instructs reachable devices to purge local product data and mailbox credentials. It retains only a nonrecoverable deletion tombstone keyed to the prior account identity, with no Product Sync or mailbox data, so a previously signed-in device is rejected and purges on reconnect instead of recreating an empty Product Account. Offline or compromised devices may retain local copies until they reconnect and process the purge or account-rejection rule. For Sign in with Apple accounts, the backend retains the token material needed to call Apple's revocation API and the deletion operation does not report completion until that token is revoked or Apple confirms it is already invalid. It never deletes mail held by a Mail Provider and does not claim to revoke provider-issued authorization, which the user may need to revoke separately. This favors a clear privacy and deletion guarantee over recovery from an accidental confirmed deletion.
