# Use local-first metadata and a bounded encrypted body cache

Mailbox views will read Durable Message Metadata from local persistence immediately while provider synchronization updates it in the background. A newly connected mailbox becomes usable after metadata for its newest 50 messages, or all provider-visible messages when fewer exist, arrives; resumable historical metadata backfill then paginates through the complete provider-visible history with separate progress, and body prefetch starts without delaying initial availability. Backfill pauses under low storage, low power, or network loss. At each synchronization reference instant, each Mailbox Connection selects at most 500 distinct messages combined across Inbox and Sent Mailbox whose applicable received or sent timestamp is on or after that instant minus 30 days. A message appearing in both roles counts once, uses the later applicable timestamp, and uses Stable Provider Message Identity as the deterministic tie-breaker. Recent bodies are admitted only when they fit without immediately evicting another selected recent body; refused bodies remain on-demand until a later synchronization finds space. Pinned message bodies remain eligible regardless of those selection cutoffs, but are admitted only when they fit without immediately evicting another pinned body. Spam, Trash, attachments, and older unpinned bodies remain on-demand. The encrypted body cache has a 500 MB device-wide hard limit and evicts opened older non-pinned bodies first, then the oldest non-pinned prefetched bodies, and finally least-recently-read pinned bodies; pin metadata survives eviction. Draft bodies use a separately encrypted 100 MB device-wide store that never evicts drafts automatically; when full, the client prevents saving additional draft content until the user removes or shortens a draft. This supersedes ADR-0005's on-demand-only body-fetch policy, trading additional device storage and background work for fast list rendering, faster message opening, and bounded offline reading.
